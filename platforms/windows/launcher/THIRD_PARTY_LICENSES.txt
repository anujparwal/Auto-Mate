SOURCE AND MODIFICATION DIRECTIONS

Bun runtime: https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/tree/bun-v1.4.4
Patched WebKit: https://github.com/oven-sh/WebKit
Bun's original 1.4.4 license explains rebuilding/relinking against its pinned
WebKit version: clone vendor/WebKit, run bun sync-webkit-source, then
bun run build:local. Follow that tagged source's dependency/build instructions.
A rebuilt compatible Bun may replace bin/bun.exe; Auto-Mate does not impose a
signature check preventing replacement of that runtime for license purposes.

Native image libraries: the bundled @img/sharp-win32-x64/versions.json records
exact library versions, including libvips 8.18.7. Corresponding upstream source
and license materials are indexed in licenses/native/. Build recipes are at
https://github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips and libvips source is at
https://github.com/libvips/libvips/releases/tag/v8.18.7 . Preserve that build's
patches/configuration when rebuilding. Interface-compatible rebuilt libvips and
companion DLLs may replace their files under node_modules/@img/sharp-win32-x64/lib;
Auto-Mate does not impose a signature check preventing those replacements.
